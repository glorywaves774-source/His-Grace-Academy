# Staff user guide

## Sign in
1. Open the local application URL provided by the administrator.
2. Enter your assigned username and temporary password.
3. If prompted, change the temporary password before proceeding.
4. Sign out using the button in the left navigation when finished.

## Student records
Authorised staff open Students, use search and filters, and open a record to view it. Staff with admission-management permissions register or update student records. Admission numbers are generated by the application when left blank. Check spelling, date of birth, guardian phone, class and status before saving. Use CSV/XLSX/PDF exports only for an authorised school purpose.

## Operational registers
Use the left navigation to open the module assigned to your role. Choose Add record, complete required fields, then save. The record is stored in the shared database. Open View / edit to update an existing record. Export CSV downloads the visible module register, subject to the module's access permissions.

- Attendance: one attendance record per student per date. Choose the correct class and status.
- Assessments: enter the score earned and maximum score. Scores above the maximum are rejected. Do not issue official report cards from this register until grading and report-card rules are approved and implemented.
- Timetable: enter 24-hour times. The application rejects overlapping sessions for the same class.
- Admissions: update the application status as the admissions team reviews the applicant. The conversion-to-student workflow is not implemented yet.
- Fees: receipts record payments received. They do not represent an invoice or outstanding balance.
- Welfare and Health: access is restricted. Record only necessary information and follow the school's safeguarding and privacy procedures.
- Documents: this is a document-reference register. It does not upload or store the actual file.

## Data quality
Use the correct date format in the form. Do not share accounts. Report suspected mistakes or duplicate records to the administrator. Do not delete or alter records to conceal an error. Ask the administrator to preserve the audit trail and correct the record.

## Known limits
Some registers are foundations rather than complete workflows. Read FEATURE_STATUS.md before relying on a module for official school decisions. SMS, email, mobile-money and USSD services are not configured.

## Taking a photo with the camera
On a student or staff form, press **Take photo with camera**, allow the browser to use the camera, then **Capture**. Use **Retake** to try again, then save the form. This needs the online (https) address; on plain http the button explains this and you can upload a file instead.

## Assigning subject teachers to JHS classes
When creating a Subject Teacher, pick the classes in **Assigned class(es)**: one class, two classes (JHS 1 & 2, JHS 2 & 3 or JHS 1 & 3) or all three. The default for a Subject Teacher is all three. The teacher can then see and work with only those classes.

## Editing and deleting users
Open **User access** and press **Edit** beside a person. Change their details and press **Save changes**; enter a new temporary password to reset their password. To remove an account, scroll to **Delete this account**, tick the confirmation and press **Delete user**. If deletion is refused because the person owns records, use **Suspend** instead. You cannot delete or change the role of your own account.

## Staff who are also parents
Open **User access → Edit** for the staff member and choose their children under **Children at the school**. After they sign in they will see **My children** in the menu, with only their own children's records. Their normal staff access does not change. To remove the link, deselect the children and save.

## Printing invoices and report cards
**Invoices:** open **Fee invoices and balances** and press **Print** beside an invoice. **Report cards:** open **Academic report cards**, choose the class, year and term, then press **Generate**. Use **Print all** for one page per pupil, or **Print this pupil** on a single card. Write the class teacher's and headteacher's remarks by hand in the lines provided, then sign and stamp.

## Subjects taught by Subject Teachers
Open **User access**, create or **Edit** the Subject Teacher, and choose their **Subjects taught** (hold Ctrl to pick several). They can then record and see results only for those subjects. Keep the subject names in **Subjects & Teaching** so the list is correct. Leave the box empty to allow any subject.

## 3D class view on the Dashboard
Open **Dashboard** and find **3D class view**. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and hover (or tap) a column to see the exact numbers. **Reset view** returns to the starting angle. **Enrolment** shows boys and girls per class; the **Fees** tab (Administrator, Proprietor and Headteacher only) shows billed, paid and outstanding per class. Choose **Table** to see the same figures as a table; this also happens automatically on devices that cannot show 3D.
